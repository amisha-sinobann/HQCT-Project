Architecture Summary

HQCT-K (Quantum Kernel)

  • Classical Q / K / V projections
  • Similarity = |⟨ψ(q)|ψ(k)⟩|²
  • 6 qubits · depth 3 (default)
  • ~14% fewer classical parameters
  • Strong few-shot advantage

HQCT-P (Quantum Projection)

  • Quantum circuits replace WQ, WK
  • Pauli-Z expectations as features
  • Value projection stays classical
  • Slightly fewer parameters than HQCT-K

Quantum Circuit

  • Angle embedding (RX + RY)
  • L layers of RX, RY, RZ
  • Nearest-neighbour CNOT chain
  • Parameter-shift differentiation
  • Near-identity initialisation
